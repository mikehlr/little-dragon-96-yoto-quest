# Arcade Brawler · 2.8

Each of the six regions now has eleven ordinary fights, its rival and its three-phase boss. Three brief automatic recovery beats replace all mandatory puzzles, deliveries, escort, defense and reading tasks. The six individual end scenes and the extra parallax layer remain. Environmental light, snow and water remain visual; there is no cold-energy drain, alarm chore or environmental objective damage.

The twenty new enemies have explicit combat profiles in `brawler-combat.js`. Approach, attack commitment, recovery and counter opportunities differ: slow golem/tree stomps, an owl's straight dive, sideways crab movement and rear vulnerability, split ink shots, a gull's landing, fish-sailor droplets, fox feint, kick-open hedgehog guard, a rolling acorn seed, circling beetle, a gargoyle's marked landing, a slowly committed bronze guard, harpy spread, accelerating scarab roll, interruptible living book, marked wax drop, a jester's bait, mouse burst and the hare's offset hop. These share readable combat primitives rather than requiring extra controls. Guarding or ordinary attacks remain useful; shields reduce damage rather than making enemies immune. Easy allows one active attacker against one player; co-op allows one more. New ordinary health is compact, and recovery hits receive a bonus.

Pending enemies do not render. Actors arrive through an actual opening door, previously visible work, native cover, rope, a moored launch/sled, or an offscreen lane. Pressure remains in small cohorts. Formation slots no longer arbitrarily inflate the learned health/size of new types. The harbor launch remains in its water berth, with a separate gangplank in front of the rim.

Street objects use compact, themed supply pockets. Native regional container art remains the same when picked up, carried or thrown. Floor details are reduced to materials that belong to each region. Shops appear once in Neighborhood, Harbor and Castle; none appear in Forest, Ruins or Frozen Pass. There are three purchases: health +50 for four coins, energy +60 for three coins, or a fresh four-use club for five coins. Coins drop from fights and supplies and collect automatically nearby. Purchases pause action, reject insufficient funds/full resources, work while carrying a weapon and use the shared co-op wallet. Rivals still award maximum-health or extra-life pickups. Wallet reset occurs when a new session actually starts, not while a cancelable region is loading.

Airborne attack swings the held club; another held object is thrown. Context can also throw a club. Jump physics continue, one attack is allowed per jump, each swing consumes one use and hits each target once. Throws inherit their release height and cannot strand a queued object when landing early. Hand coordinates are registered to the native carry poses, with a single weapon owner and real palm restoration over a carried box. No per-pose body fitting or head resizing is used.

Regional passers-by use offscreen entrances, quiet gaps and a bounded fear response. Neighborhood has people; Harbor has small gulls; Forest has foxes/hedgehogs; Ruins has beetles; Castle has mice; Frozen Pass has hares/owls. Animals use distance-driven native walk/run frames and can turn once to flee from nearby fighting. Shopkeepers are separate from incidental routes.

All foreground facades share an explicit ground support line. Source-alpha checks measure their actual contact, rather than assuming a padded image edge is the foot. Near/far production captures cover all six regions. Furniture and supply density are curated, with only a few benches at appropriate locations.
