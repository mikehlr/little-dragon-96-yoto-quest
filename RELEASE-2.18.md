# 2.18 · Arcade collection announcements

Collecting a previously unowned Yoto story card or successfully buying a souvenir now queues an automatic arcade announcement. It shows the actual item illustration, its name, regional set progress, and where it was added. Completing three regional Yoto cards announces the unlocked regional soundtrack; completing four regional souvenirs announces a complete set. A new card that also completes a three-card charge shows the team health/energy/coin reward.

Announcements appear in acquisition order, last 4.3 seconds (5.6 seconds for a complete set), then dismiss automatically. They do not change game mode, steal focus, capture pointer/touch input or require a confirmation button. The play clock keeps running. Repeated cards and rejected/duplicate purchases do not queue acquisition announcements. Pausing hides and preserves the announcement until play resumes; hiding the browser tab also suspends its timer.

The presentation uses the existing clipped collection artwork, a gilded frame, a progress line and restrained motion. Calm mode and the system reduced-motion preference disable slide transitions. Text follows Greek, English and Ukrainian, and a polite accessible status region announces the acquisition. Portrait letterbox space above the game is used when available to keep the playfield clear.

The 2.17 economy, collections, five bonus rounds and accepted campaign remain. RTM 2.16 stays untouched. See qa-results.json for the focused current validation and its scope; baseline 2.17 campaign/combat reports remain available in Git history at 375eadb1369e69f6fdfdaa06b4413db10871b7e0. No new raster artwork was generated for this change.
